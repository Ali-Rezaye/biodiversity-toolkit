How to cite

Please cite the version you used. Each tool shows its version number, and so does its page on this site.

Checklist Comparer

Rezaye A, Barkworth ME (2026). Checklist Comparer (Version v1.16) [Computer software]. https://ali-rezaye.github.io/biodiversity-toolkit/

DOI: to be added on release via Zenodo.

Phylogenetic Checklist Viewer

Rezaye A, Barkworth ME (2026). Phylogenetic Checklist Viewer (Version v49) [Computer software]. https://ali-rezaye.github.io/biodiversity-toolkit/

DOI: to be added on release via Zenodo.

Sources used by the viewer

If you publish results from the viewer, please also cite the classifications and the species counts it uses:

  • Flowering plants: APG V. Byng et al. (2026), Journal of Systematics and Evolution, doi:10.1111/jse.70096.
  • Ferns and lycophytes: PPG I (2016), Journal of Systematics and Evolution 54: 563–603.
  • Gymnosperms: Yang et al. (2022), Plant Diversity 44: 340–350.
  • Bryophytes: BPG. Li et al. (2024), Journal of Systematics and Evolution 62: 577–788. The order of the three bryophyte phyla follows Zhang et al. (2020), Nature Plants 6: 107–118.
  • World species counts: Catalogue of Life, COL26.7 XR (17 July 2026), doi:10.48580/dgykv.
