How to use the Checklist Cleaner

The Checklist Cleaner applies one table of name corrections to any number of checklists at once and gives back corrected copies, with a report of every change.

Version v1.1. One HTML file, about 60 KB. Runs offline in an up-to-date web browser.

Open in browser Download the file

What it does

You give it a two-column correction table and your checklist files. Column 1 is a name as it appears in your files; column 2 is the replacement. An empty column 2 deletes every row carrying that name. If the two headings are ranks, such as genus and subfamily, it instead adds a subfamily column filled from the genus.

A renamed genus is renamed in every species of that genus; a renamed species also corrects its genus column. Chains (A → B, B → C) are followed to the end; a table that contradicts itself is refused with the row numbers.

When two rows end up with the same name, they are merged: every count column is added up and the submitted names are joined. Rows that were already duplicates are merged too, and reported separately.

Your original files are never changed: the corrected copies go into a zip, with CHANGES.csv (every change: file, row, column, old, new, what happened), SUMMARY.csv (one line per file), the table as applied, and the table rows that matched nothing.

Your results in three steps

  1. Press “Load correction table (CSV)…” and choose your table.
  2. Press “Add checklists…” and choose your files.
  3. Press “Run”, read the summary, and download the zip.

A “Try the example” button shows the whole process on two small invented lists.

Preparing your data

The table is a CSV with a header row and two columns (old name, new name). Names may carry authorship; it is ignored when matching.

The checklists are CSV files with the names in columns headed by their rank (family, genus, species or scientificName).

What it does not do

It does not decide which name is right. Check names first with ChecklistBank (any group) or TNRS (plants) and build the table from the result.

A table applies to every file loaded, so it cannot delete a row from one file only.

Downloads

Everything can be downloaded: the zip described above, and the summary and the changes also as separate CSV files.
