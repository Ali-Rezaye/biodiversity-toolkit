Biodiversity Toolkit

Free tools for people who work with biodiversity data: some for working with the data that biodiversity studies produce, some for learning the vocabulary those studies use, and some for doing the research better. Each tool is one file that opens in your browser; most run offline, and nothing you load is sent anywhere.

Checklist Comparer

Calculates the similarity of two or more lists of taxa from any taxonomic group based on the taxa they include. It enables use of three similarity indices and presents the results as similarity matrices and cluster diagrams that group the lists using three algorithms. Viewing results from different combinations of indices and algorithms is quick and easy.

Phylogenetic Checklist Viewer

Summarizes the families present in a list of plant taxa based on their phylogenetic, not alphabetical, order. Can show which families are not present in a list and, for those that are, the absolute or relative abundance of the orders, families, genera, and species present. It can also compare two or more checklists in the same manner as the Checklist Comparer.

Checklist Cleaner

Applies a table of name corrections — synonyms, misspellings, deletions, or a new rank such as subfamily — to any number of checklists at once, merges the records of a synonym into its accepted name, reports every change, and returns the corrected files.

Why they are built this way

Most work offline

Every tool published so far runs without an internet connection once downloaded: in the field, in the herbarium, or where the connection is slow. A tool that needs a connection will say so on its page.

Nothing to install

There is no program to install and no account to create. Double-click the file and it opens in your browser.

Nothing is uploaded

Your files stay on your computer. The tools read them inside the browser and send nothing anywhere, unless a tool's page says otherwise.
