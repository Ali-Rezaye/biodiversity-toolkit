About

The Checklist Comparer, the Phylogenetic Checklist Viewer and the Checklist Cleaner were written by Ali Rezaye and Mary E. Barkworth. A paper describing them is in preparation.

Privacy

This website counts visits with GoatCounter: no cookies, no personal data. It records the page, the country, the browser and the kind of device, and discards the visitor’s address. Pressing a download button records the tool and version downloaded; giving an email address is optional. The tools themselves send nothing anywhere, online or offline.
